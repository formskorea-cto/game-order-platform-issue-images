piggyback.glb is a modified derivative of the Cesium Man mesh and skeleton.
Changes: Original logo texture removed. Added eyes, nose, mouth, ears and hair.
Changed body proportions, materials and clothing. Added a second adult with
folded legs, shoulder-holding arms and a fitted jersey. Created new in-place
load-bearing idle and running animations with two-joint leg IK, support hands,
linear foot stance paths, bent-knee recovery and brief suspension phases.

The Cesium logo and trademarks are not included in this derivative.
The original model's animation is not used in the game's run_loop.

Locomotion follow-up, 2026-10-17:
- Added a second articulated 19-bone passenger skeleton.
- Replaced the rigid upper-back attachment with anatomical skin binding.
- Kept shoulder grips and thigh support through separate two-joint IK solves.
- Added restrained delayed torso response and hanging-calf movement.
- Authored secondary amplitudes; these are not measured adult-running data.
